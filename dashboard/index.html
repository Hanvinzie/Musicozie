Instrument

Changing the instrument stops any note that is sounding.

How to Play ? open / close
  1. Right hand = melody. Form one of the 8 gestures below; the note sounds for as long as you hold the gesture.
  2. Left hand = minor modifier. With the left hand idle or in any gesture other than thumb only (10000), notes stay major (C scale).
  3. Minor Toggle: while the left hand shows only the thumb open, every right-hand gesture plays 1 semitone lower (minor / flat). Release the thumb to return to major.
  4. Fist (00000) or an unknown gesture = silence.
  5. Switch instrument: hold a fist with both hands for 1.5 seconds (or click the Piano / Violin button).

NOTE GESTURES (ONE HAND, 5 FINGERS)

Diagram: T = thumb, I = index, M = middle, R = ring, P = pinky. White = open, empty = closed. The row being detected right now lights up green (amber while Minor is active).
Tips: face your palm to the camera, keep the whole hand in view, use good lighting and stay about 40-60 cm away. Hold a gesture for about 0.1 s (debounce) so the note changes cleanly. The left hand alone (without the right hand) does not play.
